Candidate guidance

Assessment Instructions

Read every instruction before beginning. Section timers operate independently and expired sections become locked.

Before you start

  • Use a stable internet connection and keep the device charged.
  • Close unnecessary browser tabs and applications.
  • Use the latest Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox.
  • Keep approximately 80 minutes available without interruption.
  • Enter accurate candidate information during registration.

Assessment format

The assessment contains 102 questions, carries 115 marks and is organised across five sections.

Timer rule: Every section has its own timer. If one section expires, that section locks. The assessment submits automatically only after all section timers have expired.

Autosave and recovery

Answers are saved locally in the browser. Returning on the same device and browser may offer recovery after an unexpected interruption.

Important: Do not clear browser data, switch browsers, or change devices during an active attempt.

Submission

Use Finish Assessment when ready and keep the confirmation visible until submission completes.
