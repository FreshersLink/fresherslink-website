About FreshersLink.in

Built by engineers, strengthened by industry experience.

What the assessment covers

Five dimensions of readiness

Each section is presented separately so candidates can focus while monitoring progress and time.

A

Aptitude

Quantitative reasoning, logical thinking and problem solving.

T

Technical Readiness

Core engineering and employability-oriented technical understanding.

C

Communication

Workplace communication, comprehension and professional interaction.

P

Professional Skills

Decision-making, reliability, teamwork and workplace judgement.

S

Self-awareness

Behavioural preferences and professional-development indicators.

✓

Readiness Insight

A consolidated view to guide further improvement and preparation.
